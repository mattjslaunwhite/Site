MAGIC GOAT STUDIO PORTAL

Welcome to the digital workshop of Matt-Réal Slaunwhite in Canada. This portal brings together a WebAssembly x86 hypervisor, interactive retro operating system recreations, an offline ZIM wiki archive reader, AES-256 encrypted live comms, custom 3D-printed UAV airframes, off-grid 24V solar telemetry, and browser-native hardware tools—all running straight from the edge with zero bloat.
