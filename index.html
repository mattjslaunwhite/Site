MAGIC GOAT STUDIO PORTAL

Welcome to the digital workshop of Matt-Réal Slaunwhite in Woodstock, New Brunswick. This portal brings together interactive retro operating system recreations, custom 3D-printed UAV airframes, off-grid 24V solar telemetry, and browser-native hardware tools—all running straight from the edge with zero bloat.
