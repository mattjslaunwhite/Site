🧭 Adventurer's Field Guide

🏠 Enter Cottages & Shops
Walk up to any cottage door in Willowbrook or Port Brioche (or press [E]) to step inside, rest, chat, and find hidden chests!
🪄 Staff & Tall Grass
Space / Z / Left-Click swings your Starlight Staff and cuts tall grass for hidden Hearts & Gold Gems.
🍅 Barnaby's Tomato Hat
X / Right-Click lobs an arcing Heirloom Tomato that splashes and slows foes. Press Q for Purr-Bloom Heal!
🌱 Lost Starter Companion
Rescue Sproutling in southern Whisperwood to unlock [R] Sprout-Burst and earn a Heart Container from Prof. Maple!
